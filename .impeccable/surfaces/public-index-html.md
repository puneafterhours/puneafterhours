# Home

Operate. AfterHours and r/PuneTonight, equal weight. Every job is obvious on a phone. Not a startup page, not a product with customers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Four equal jobs as flat color pans. The page refuses a hero, a rounded display face, and a newspaper.

OWN-WORLD: White ground. Birthday is magenta #c2185b with white type, Wall is turmeric #f0b400 with ink #1c1914, Proposal is indigo #3949ab with white type, What's on is green #2e7d32 with white type. The pan you are on has a 4px black rim and the others do not. One face, Zilla Slab, 700 for names and 500 for facts, no italics. Buttons are black pills. The current link is a magenta chip. Nothing flashes. No rules between the pans, only the gap.

STORY: A person opening the shared link sees Birthday, Wall, Proposal, and What's on at once, each a solid pan with one fact and one action. They can add a birthday, read the wall, open a proposal that stays marked not locked, or see a source named unavailable. Privacy choices stay visible on the form. Nothing is pitched.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A white bar. "PuneAfterhours" in Zilla Slab, five links, Home as a magenta chip. One lede. Then four pans. On a phone they stack and still fit with the five links. Birthday wears the black rim until another pan is hovered or focused, and the rim moves there. Each pan has its heading, one fact, one short line, and a black pill. Proposal says "Not locked" in the pan's own type. What's on names a source that is down, in words.

FORM: Holi pans set in a slab, fourth on the type list. seed:c0f8975e61539d6c

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
